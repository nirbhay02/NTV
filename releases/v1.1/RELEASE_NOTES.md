## Highlights & Changes in v1.1
1. **Rebranded to NTV**:
   - Official app title and manifest label changed to **NTV**.
   - Updated top dashboard branding to display the new NTV logo and title.
   - Updated all navigational headers (`TvTopBar`), settings, connection indicators, and setup dialogs.
   - Updated user-facing strings across English and Hindi locales.
2. **New Modern NTV Logo & TV Banner**:
   - High-definition stylized glowing neon 'N' wave streaming logo with dark carbon obsidian backdrop.
   - Android TV Leanback launcher banner (`tv_banner.png` 320x180 & 640x360).
   - High-resolution adaptive launcher icons (`ic_launcher`, `ic_launcher_round`, `ic_fg`) across all density buckets (mdpi, hdpi, xhdpi, xxhdpi, xxxhdpi).
3. **Dedicated Cinematic Splash Screen**:
   - Smooth animated entrance with scale and fade animations.
   - Real-time engine & channel initialization status indicator.
   - Seamless auto-transition to the Live TV Dashboard with remote key skip support.
4. **Theme & Engine Updates**:
   - Added `NtvTheme` and `Theme.NTVAndroidTV`.
   - Updated internal configuration title and user-facing status logs.
